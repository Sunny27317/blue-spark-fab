# Blue Spark Forge

Build a complete, premium, production-quality website for a UK business called:

BLUE SPARK

Catering Equipment & Metal Fabrication

BUSINESS POSITIONING

Blue Spark is a Leeds-based metal fabrication and catering-equipment company specialising in:

- Bespoke stainless-steel fabrication

- Commercial catering equipment

- Stainless-steel tables and workstations

- Commercial cookers and grills

- Gates

- Fences

- Huks / security grilles

- Bespoke metalwork

- Kitchen equipment repairs

- Custom fabrication for restaurants, takeaways, commercial kitchens and other businesses

Core brand message:

“Built Strong. Made to Last.”

Secondary brand message:

“Precision in Every Detail, Quality in Every Build.”

The company should feel:

- highly skilled

- trustworthy

- local

- industrial

- premium

- durable

- experienced

- capable of completely bespoke work

DO NOT create a generic SaaS-looking website.

DO NOT make it look like a cheap template.

DO NOT overuse gradients, glassmorphism or unnecessary animations.

DO NOT make it look like an online appliance supermarket.

The website should feel like a modern British engineering/fabrication company.

==================================================

DESIGN DIRECTION

==================================================

Create a bold premium industrial visual identity inspired by:

- brushed stainless steel

- dark workshop environments

- welding sparks

- deep navy

- electric blue

- black/charcoal

- clean white typography

Use the attached Blue Spark marketing artwork as the main brand reference.

Suggested palette:

- Midnight navy: #041427

- Deep blue: #071D3A

- Electric blue: #006BFF

- Bright accent blue: #159BFF

- Charcoal: #111820

- Steel grey: #BFC5CB

- Light silver: #E7E9EB

- White: #FFFFFF

The design should have excellent contrast.

Use the electric blue only as an accent:

- CTA buttons

- hover states

- icons

- small highlights

- section dividers

Avoid turning the whole site blue.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use a strong contemporary sans-serif font such as:

- Manrope

- Inter

- Plus Jakarta Sans

or another premium geometric sans-serif.

Headlines should feel powerful and industrial.

Use bold weights and tight line spacing.

Do not use script fonts except perhaps very subtly for one decorative phrase if needed.

The website must remain professional.

ANIMATION

==================================================

Use motion sparingly.
